1. Regional Performance Analysis

The geographical analysis shows that sales and profit vary across different regions. Some regions generate higher sales and contribute significantly to the overall business revenue, while other regions show comparatively lower sales and profit. This indicates that business performance is not uniform across all geographical locations. Analysing regional variations helps identify strong and weak markets and understand differences in customer demand and purchasing behaviour.

2. State-wise Sales Performance

The state-wise geographical map highlights differences in sales contributions among various states. Certain states contribute a larger share of total sales, indicating strong customer demand and better business performance. Other states generate relatively lower sales, suggesting opportunities for improvement. This analysis helps businesses identify important sales locations and understand how geographical factors influence overall revenue.

3. City-wise Sales Distribution

The city-wise sales visualization shows that sales are concentrated in particular cities, while some cities contribute comparatively less to total revenue. Cities with larger sales values indicate stronger market demand and better business opportunities. The analysis helps identify major sales-generating cities and locations where additional marketing and customer engagement strategies may be required.
4. Recommendations
1. Improve Low-performing Regions

The business should focus on improving sales performance in regions that generate lower revenue or experience negative profits. Effective marketing campaigns, promotional offers, customer engagement activities and better distribution strategies can help attract more customers. Businesses should also analyse the reasons behind low profitability, such as high operational costs, discounts or shipping expenses, and take suitable corrective actions.

2. Strengthen High-performing Cities

Businesses should focus on strengthening their presence in cities that generate higher sales and profits. Maintaining sufficient product availability, improving delivery services and expanding distribution networks can help meet increasing customer demand. Companies can also introduce targeted promotional campaigns and customer loyalty programmes to retain existing customers and attract new buyers.
